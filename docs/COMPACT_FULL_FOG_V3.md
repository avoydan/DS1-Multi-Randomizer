# Compact Fog v3 and generation profile `ds1tr-gen-v6`

`ds1tr-gen-v6` (annotation source `compact-v3`, catalog `ds1tr-compact-full-fog-v3`) fixes the original Fog hard-lock at its root: a start region that the solver believed was connected to the world, but physically was not. It replaces the Ceaseless-specific post-boss route check of v5 with a general start-reachability rule, and (before release) corrects two further physically unsound fixed routes found by the 11-route audit: the Lost Izalith lava crossing and the Sen's Fortress cage, and the unconditional links back into boss arenas (the owner's Quelaag trap). V1 to V5 are untouched and replay exactly as before. Nothing here has been played in the game yet; see "What is not proven".

## The original hard-lock

Source evidence: support bundle `DS1TR-support-06NWCQQ3-20261020-181751Z.zip` (profile `ds1tr-gen-v4`, master seed 332055808, Fog seed 4009337993, Fog attempt 0), the 2026-10-20 diagnosis (`dcc5848`) and a replay of the frozen v1 catalog graph for that seed.

- The Asylum starting hallway (Area `asylum`) is separated from Oscar's cell by a randomizable wall, `o8952_0000`. The wall is the hallway's only randomized exit.
- In the original layout the wall paired with the Ceaseless boss pocket (`demonruins_ceaseless`), whose only randomized exit (`o4961_0000`) returns to the hallway: a closed two-area loop. The player enters, can only walk back, and nothing else is reachable.
- FogMod's solver accepted the layout because the project's own v1 and v2 catalog gave the hallway **two unconditional fixed exits** that upstream FogMod does not have: `asylum > asylum_outside` (made by `AddLocalSequence`) and `asylum > firelink` (a `MapRoute` called the tutorial departure). Upstream FogMod conditions the first on having visited the balcony and Oscar's cell and has no hallway route to Firelink (the crow departure is the fixed or randomized warp of `asylum_loop`).
- The 2026-10-20 diagnosis blamed a second false edge, an unconditional `demonruins_ceaseless > demonruins_lava` route. Removing that edge alone changes nothing for this seed: the hallway's own false exits still make every Area look reachable. The later Ceaseless post-boss route work (`7766d69`, `a24cc36`, the v5 validator) therefore addressed a different problem. It rejects the original loop only incidentally, because the pocket's exit area has no fixed route to the lava region.

Quantified on the frozen catalogs, reading the connected graph with "no unconditional fixed exit leaves the hallway" (raw Fog seeds, the five original options, scratch measurement of 2026-11-01): 53.5% of 200 v4 layouts and 45.7% of 300 v5 layouts leave the player in a closed start region (42.0% and 36.0% severe, 15 Areas or fewer). The Ceaseless pocket was one of many dead-end destinations. Of the 14 v5 worlds the Ceaseless check accepted in that sample, 5 were start-trapped by this reading. The V5 owner smoke seed (Fog seed 6843) and the V5 support-reproduction golden are among them.

## What v6 changes

1. **Catalog v3** (`FogCompactCatalogBuilder.BuildV3`, built from the unchanged v2 output): the hallway keeps no unconditional exit. `asylum > asylum_outside` requires the balcony and Oscar's cell to have been visited; the hallway-owned `asylum > firelink` route is removed (the crow warp of `asylum_loop` remains the departure, with and without area warps). Catalog construction fails if the hallway ever gains an unconditional exit. All other links are v2's. The v2 fingerprint `73f3c842...` is unchanged; v3 is `1e7188b128054e2c87e9e6389634309e5d1c6fbfbeabffbf57cc679f95b191ac`.
2. **The rule lives in the graph, not in a stricter runtime check.** FogMod's connector repairs layouts for up to 100 tries against what the graph says. With a physically correct hallway it steers the Asylum wall toward a region that reaches the world, instead of the validator rejecting after the fact.
3. **`StartReachabilityValidator`** (v6 only) re-asserts after the connector that every Area is reachable from the start Area. This single statement covers start traps, terminal pockets and dead-end cycles for any region or boss. A failure is the same retryable `FOG_SEED_UNSOLVABLE` (`solve-graph`, scope `fog`) as the connector's own failures.
4. **The Ceaseless post-boss route proof is retired for v6.** Its boss-completion rule no longer requires a fixed post-boss route, and `BossProgressionValidator` is not called for `compact-v3`. The lower Demon Ruins route stays an ordinary boss-completion edge (`demonruins > demonruins_lava` gated by event `11410410`) and the default landing fix for Ceaseless is kept. `compact-v2` (v5) still runs its frozen check.
5. **Two more routes corrected (see "Lava crossing and Sen's cage").** Both lava entries need the Orange Charred Ring; the Sen's cage door needs the Cage Key and the cage no longer joins `sens_mid` to `sens_upper`; the two keys are found where the game puts them (ring in `demonruins_centipede`, cage key in `sens_upper`).
6. **Boss arenas have no free way back in (see "Boss arena entry").** A post-encounter neighbour can only walk back into a boss arena after the arena has been reached, which in practice means after an arrival through the boss's own fog.
7. **Host compatibility identifier unchanged.** `Program.HostVersion` stays `0.7.3`; v6 is selected by its annotation source and catalog fingerprint, so no earlier profile's allow-list changed. A v6 request is refused by an older host as an unknown annotation source. The FogHost project version is `0.8.0`.
8. **The retry budget is unchanged** (3 combined, 5 Fog attempts).

## Lava crossing and Sen's cage

Both came from `AddLocalSequence` (`FogAnnotationPolicy.cs`), which links consecutive names of a list with unconditional two-way fixed routes. Evidence and decisions (2026-11-02):

**Orange Charred Ring and the lava (Demon Ruins / Lost Izalith).**
- Upstream FogMod gates both entries into the lava segment (`demonruins_izalith > demonruins_izalithlava` and `demonruins_izalithprelava > demonruins_izalithlava`) on the ring and leaves both exits free. The v1 and v2 catalogs have the same structure without the condition, although `ResolveV2OrangeCharredRing` already describes the ring as "the lava-gate condition".
- Which acquisition to model: item lot 2670 is the real one. Pristine event 11415386 (`m14_01_00_00`) awards lot 2670 once eleven characters are dead: the Centipede Demon (`c5200`, entity 1410700) and its ten spawn (`c5201`, `c5202`), all on collision `h0062B1`, which upstream maps to the Area `demonruins_centipede` (upstream `LotLocations`: `2670: demonruins_centipede`). Lot 4030 is a dormant relief treasure (event "treasure for rescue use 03": no part, start disabled), not an acquisition. The earlier catalog took the first lot's coarse Area, `demonruins`, which put the ring outside the Centipede encounter.
- Representation: the graph models availability, not storage. The ring is a `RandomOnly` key item found in the Area of its source lot; v3 sets the area of lot 2670 to `demonruins_centipede`, which is fixed-adjacent to `demonruins_izalithprelava`, so the prelava entry is always satisfiable from its own side. The crossing is a real requirement only for a player who reaches Lost Izalith first (through a randomized gate), which is exactly the case v1 to v5 accepted for free (7.6% to 30% of sampled layouts relied on it).
- Physical necessity: upstream's author models it as required and the project's own V2 note agrees. The ring's effect could not be read independently (`EquipParamAccessory` has no layout in the project's param snapshot), so "required to cross" is not proven from game data. The correction only adds a condition, which restricts layouts; it cannot create a hard-lock.

**Cage Key and the Sen's cage.**
- Upstream: `sens_mid > sens_cage` is a key door (Cage Key), `sens_cage` has no exits at all, and the key lies in `sens_upper`. The real mid-to-roof link is the randomizable wall `o5153_0000`.
- The cage key is item lot 1500150, a treasure on collision `h0032B0_0000`, an upper-Sen's collision (upstream `LotLocations`: `1500150: sens_upper`, collision table: `h0032B0_0000` is `sens_upper`). The earlier catalog took the coarse Area `sens`. The project key declaration put the key in `sens_cage` itself, which would be circular once the door is locked. Lot 4002 (the other Cage Key lot) is an orphan with no treasure, event or drop reference and is not in the project's lot index.
- The cage link to the roof is a list-order artifact: the sequence `sens, sens_mid, sens_cage, sens_upper, sens_irongolem` chained `sens_mid > sens_cage > sens_upper`, and the policy gate pair (`sens_mid`, `sens_upper`, the wall `o5153_0000`) was only skipped for consecutive names, so the cage bypassed it. In about 30% of sampled layouts the graph relied on the cage to reach the roof, and in 12.8% of 1000 layouts the roof (where the key lies) was reachable only through the unlocked cage.
- Is the cage-to-roof transit physically real? No evidence for it: upstream has none, the project's own gate-pair policy says mid and roof join only through the wall, and the only data link between the two is the key's pickup collision. It is not proven by map geometry either, so v3 removes it (and keeps the way back out of the cage). Once the door is open it stays open, but it only gives access to the cage room.

Not changed: `burg_upper > burg_upperresidence` (route 4 of the audit). The residence key's source lot 6231 is awarded by common event 960 for NPC 6230, which upstream does not annotate, and the lot's Area (`burg_start`) differs from upstream's key area (`burg_upper`) with no clear correction in the data; it stays a known, low-severity limitation (about 1.5% of sampled layouts).

## Boss arena entry

Source evidence: the owner's V6 world (history entry of 2026-11-01 21:45 local, master seed 2825961829, Fog seed 4038375671, Fog attempt 0, catalog fingerprint `6849fb69...`, i.e. the catalog before this rule) and a replay of it.

- The owner's Asylum wall (`o8952_0000`) paired with the main Demon Ruins side of the Ceaseless fog. Demon Ruins reaches Quelaag's bell room (`blighttown_bell`) by a fixed route, the bell room linked back into Quelaag's arena (`blighttown_quelaag`) by an unconditional fixed link, and the arena's randomized boss-side fog (`o4950_0000`) led to `sens_mid`, the rest of the world. The solver accepted the layout. In the game the only onward fog was the back of Quelaag's fog with Quelaag alive, which does not open from that side.
- With the arena's randomized exit unusable from inside, the owner's start reaches four Areas (Asylum, Demon Ruins, the bell room, the arena) and 123 are unreachable.
- Cause: `AddLocalSequence` never links INTO a boss Area ("an ordinary Area.To edge cannot bypass its entrance"), but `AddLocalRoute` is two-way, so every boss Area that owns a post-encounter route also received the reverse link from that neighbour back into the arena, unconditionally. 12 boss Areas have such a link. The v5 boss-completion machinery cannot express this for Quelaag: its boss-side entrance carries trigger-volume metadata and no matched completion fact (the completion rule only exists for Ceaseless, Artorias and the Gaping Dragon).
- Rule (catalog v3 only): a back-entry link carries the condition "the arena Area has been reached". The only other way into a boss Area is its own fog, so the link works after an arrival through the gate and never before. The link out of the arena stays free. Forward-only encounter routes (Gwyndolin, the hard Gaping Dragon drop) have no reverse link and are not touched. The rule lives in the graph, so the connector (up to 100 repair tries) steers away from the trap before the start-reachability validator would reject it.
- The twelve links: `burg_posttaurus > burg_taurus`, `parish_bell > parish_gargoyles`, `darkroot_postbutterfly > darkroot_butterfly`, `dlc_sanctuary > dlc_guardian`, `dlc_hallway > dlc_artorias`, `dlc_township > dlc_kalameetdeath`, `dlc_top > dlc_manus`, `blighttown_bell > blighttown_quelaag`, `demonruins_stairs > demonruins_firesage`, `demonruins_izalithprelava > demonruins_centipede`, `anorlondo_start > sens_irongolem`, `newlondo_drakes > newlondo_fourkings`.
- Scope of the evidence: only Quelaag is confirmed in the game (owner observation). The other eleven share the construction and the project's own stated policy, and were restricted for that reason. In 300 raw Fog seeds on the catalog without the rule, 290 accepted layouts used at least one of the twelve links before its arena had been reached by another route (Quelaag 127, Iron Golem 119, Sanctuary Guardian 111, Four Kings 108, Demon Firesage 101, Taurus 90, Manus 85, Kalameet 83; Gargoyles, Moonlight Butterfly, Artorias and Centipede 0). That measures how much the free links mattered to the solver, not how many worlds are physically trapped. With the rule the same 300 seeds are all accepted by the connector and the validator, and none depends on a back-entry.
- Not established: whether killing Quelaag from inside after walking in from the bell room opens her fog (the fight may not start from that side). The rule is conservative about it: arrival through the gate is what counts.

## What is not proven

- No v6 world has been played. The start-trap diagnosis is a model-based finding; the quickest in-game confirmation is to load the V5 owner smoke code (`DS1TR1-06NWCQQ3-K1HD8E66-2S3AD5N6-CTHZBKB8-FMSNNQWC-TRVW78NY-040F30XX-F0`, which stays V5) and look at where the first Asylum wall leads, then play the V6 smoke code.
- Only the Asylum start, the lava crossing, the Sen's cage and the twelve boss-arena back-entries were corrected (the back-entries are confirmed in the game for Quelaag only; the rule is a restriction, so it cannot create a hard-lock, but it was not played through for the other eleven). The comparison of the project's fixed Area routes with upstream FogMod's `Area.To` list found 11 routes the project made unconditional where upstream has a condition; three are now fixed (both lava entries and the cage door). Of the other eight, the 11-route audit classified six as safe (no layout of 1000 depends on them: the chandelier, both Burg lower and DLC hidden-area leaves, Gough, the crystal golem, the Drakes/Beatrice route and the prelava entry now covered by the lava fix), the Asylum F2 west route as a deliberate stand-in (the F2 west key is not on the Asylum exit path; the project routes the unrandomized Asylum Demon door through it, and conditioning it would recreate the start trap), and the upper Burg residence as a low-severity limitation. A further 157 project-only fixed routes were not cross-checked (upstream expresses many map links through entrances and warps instead). Any of these can still make the solver optimistic mid-game: residual risk and a follow-up.
- Removing the Ceaseless post-boss check relies on the topology argument: boss completion is a persistent event flag, the Ceaseless pocket is a leaf, and the lava route belongs to main Demon Ruins. No gameplay trace shows that route being needed or unneeded.
